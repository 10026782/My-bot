## When would I actually use this?

1. **BentoPDF:** When a contract, listing pack, scanned receipt, or CV needs merging, compression, OCR, or page cleanup immediately.
2. **VERT:** When a document or image is in the wrong format and the source file should stay on the device.
3. **CyberChef:** When an encoded value, hash, text export, or messy structured snippet needs inspection or conversion.
4. **SQL for Files:** When a CSV/JSON export is too large or awkward for spreadsheet filtering, joins, or quick aggregation.
5. **Squoosh:** When listing or campaign images are too large for a portal, email, or WhatsApp.
6. **ShareClean:** Before pasting logs, configs, or copied data into a support ticket, Slack, or AI assistant.
7. **Metadata Remover:** Before publishing a phone photo or document that might reveal GPS, author, device, or editing metadata.
8. **PairDrop:** When a file needs to move from phone to laptop quickly without creating a new account.
9. **SVGOMG:** When a logo or icon works visually but is bloated or contains unnecessary SVG data.
10. **RAWGraphs:** When a manager needs a quick chart from a CSV and building a dashboard would take longer than the decision.
11. **csv.repair:** When an export opens incorrectly, has broken quoting/rows, or fails an import.
12. **Mr. Data Converter:** When someone needs to turn a spreadsheet export into JSON/XML for a handoff or review.
13. **JSON Crack:** When a nested API response or configuration is hard to understand as plain text.
14. **QrCodeShow:** When a printed flyer, sign, listing sheet, or business card needs a scannable link.
15. **Paper Knife:** When a non-sensitive PDF must be merged or compressed quickly and local processing is not required.
16. **Screenshot Studio:** When a raw app/listing screenshot needs a presentable visual for a proposal or post.
17. **Planar:** When a plan, image, or notice must print at exact physical dimensions across multiple sheets.
18. **Pandoc WASM:** When a short Markdown brief must become a PDF or another document format without installing a converter.
19. **Ul0:** When a campaign or listing needs a shorter link with a QR code and basic click measurement.
20. **EasyPeasyCV:** When preparing a CV quickly and keeping control of the candidate’s information matters.

## Privacy/data-upload concerns

Paper Knife, CV builders, hosted screenshot tools, short-link services, and any entry marked **NEEDS VERIFICATION**. Do not use them with contracts, identity documents, candidate records, private property documents, customer exports, or credentials until their processing model is confirmed.

## Possible future SCOREBOS shortcuts

JSON Crack, Ul0, and a curated subset of the bookmark-now list could eventually appear as static Business Toolbox links. Integration is not justified: the value is immediate direct use, and SCOREBOS should not become a file-processing proxy or a second document system.
